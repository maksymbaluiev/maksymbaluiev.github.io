Black Daws is a Ukrainian women's ethno-group founded by migrants in Zurich. They perform traditional Ukrainian songs, blending authentic vocals with modern theatrical collaborations. Their performances build a cultural bridge between Ukraine and the world.

For this project, I developed the logo, brand graphics, printed materials, and social media design for the music group "Black Daws."

The band was founded during Russia's full-scale war against Ukraine, with one of its key missions being to counter Russian propaganda — especially claims denying the existence of Ukrainian culture, which in reality has deep, centuries-old roots.
