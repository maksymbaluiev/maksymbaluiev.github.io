Meet Slice 2.0 Typeface, a unique and versatile variable display font inspired by pixel art and digital aesthetics. Designed to be highly adaptable, Slice allows you to stretch and manipulate its forms, making it perfect for bold headlines, experimental posters, retro-gaming designs, and modern digital branding.

Whether you're creating a tech-focused identity or a creative editorial piece, Slice Typeface brings a distinct, edgy character to your work.

**Key features**

- **Variable font:** stretch and adjust the width and weight to fit your design needs perfectly.
- **Display and pixel aesthetic:** a strong, blocky structure that commands attention.
- **Extensive language support:** Latin (Basic, Western European, Central European, South East European, South American) and Cyrillic, including full support for Ukrainian.
- **100% free:** available for both personal and commercial use (©2026).
